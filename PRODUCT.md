# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People with print-export PDFs who need to remove crop marks from one document or a batch. They need to inspect the results and download clean copies without creating an account or uploading their documents to a server.

## Product purpose

trim-crop-marks is a Bootpack Digital product. It detects PDF crop marks or embedded trim boundaries, offers before-and-after previews, and downloads individual PDFs or a ZIP. All processing stays on the user's device.

## Brand personality

Approachable, capable, and practical. Use Bootpack Digital's existing identity and direct voice. The reference is the live Bootpack Digital website and the adjacent bootpack-digital repository, including its documented brand personality.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic branding disconnected from Bootpack Digital. The previous cream, olive, and rust palette does not represent the company. Avoid decorative changes that make the upload, preview, or download workflow harder to use.

## Design principles

- Make the PDF workflow obvious.
- Identify Bootpack Digital as the provider and link to its website.
- Use the established Bootpack identity consistently.
- Keep privacy and processing status clear.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Maintain the existing automated accessibility checks, keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, reduced-motion support, and mobile layouts without horizontal overflow. Target WCAG AA text contrast.
