# Design

## Reference

Use https://bootpackdigital.com/ and the adjacent bootpack-digital repository's src/app.css and brand assets as the source of truth.

## Colors

Bootpack navy #003856 anchors headings and primary actions. Blue #2787b7 and #005d8f support the identity. Coral #f06052 is the logo accent. Small coral text uses a darker variation of the site's #c65047 to meet AA contrast on pale blue. Pale blue #f5f9fb and #e9f3f8 provide page and upload surfaces. Keep success, warning, and error indicators distinct and labeled.

## Typography

Use locally served Figtree Variable, matching Bootpack Digital. Bold navy headings and clear body text replace the previous lightweight Inter styling.

## Layout and components

Preserve the current PDF workflow and responsive breakpoints. Use the product name in the header and the official horizontal Bootpack logo in the provider section at the end of the main content. Use navy primary buttons and blue focus rings. Do not load remote fonts or images during PDF processing.
